AnimeVocab is installed

Three things and you are learning. The first one is watching anime.

  1. 1

    Press play on something Japanese

    Open an episode with Japanese audio or Japanese subtitles. AnimeVocab reads the subtitle track as it goes — nothing to set up per show.

  2. 2

    Your first card appears on its own

    One word per line, in romaji, chosen for the level you are actually at. Hover or click a subtitle word to look it up, or open the toolbar popup to start Listening Mode and the Copilot on the tab you are watching.

  3. 3

    Connect your account

    Linking backs your words up to the cloud, keeps them on every browser you sign in to, and turns on Listening Mode and the AI coach. Already signed in on animevocab.com in this Chrome profile? Then this is already done.

Nothing you watch leaves this browser except the words you save. ·
