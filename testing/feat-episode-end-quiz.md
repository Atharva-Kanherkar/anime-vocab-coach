# feat/episode-end-quiz — Test Contract

When a learner finishes an episode (any show, outside the N5 course), the
extension offers a short quiz on what they just watched. It works the same for
both ways of learning: subtitle "Learn" (page subtitles and the player's own
track) and Listening Mode (lines heard from the audio). Every line either path
hands to `onLine` is quiz material.

The quiz is free (new value; nothing that is free today moves behind Pro). Haru,
the tutor, is the Pro/Max layer: Haru reads the lines aloud, judges a sentence
the learner writes, and gives a short spoken verdict.

## Functional Behavior

### Collecting what was watched (`src/lib/episode-quiz.ts`, `EpisodeLog`)
- Every line `onLine` accepts is noted with its context (English) line, deduped
  by text, per session (`currentSessionId()`); a new session starts a new log.
- Capped at 400 lines; when sampled for the request, lines are evenly spread
  across the episode (max 120 sent), not just the last ones.
- Playback time is counted only while the video is playing (1 s ticks).

### When the episode ends (`shouldOfferQuiz`)
Offer once per session when ALL hold:
- not opted out (`avcEpisodeQuizOff !== true`), not already offered for this
  session key (persisted in `avcEpisodeQuizAsked`, newest 50 keys kept);
- not in the N5 course (`course` set) — the course has its own checkpoint (#202);
- at least 20 distinct lines and 8 minutes of actual playback in this session;
- the episode is ending: `video.ended`, or duration ≥ 8 min and the time left is
  ≤ min(150 s, 10% of duration). Seeking to the end without watching does not
  qualify (playback minimum).

### The nudge
- Small card bottom-right (shadow DOM, key-shielded, follows fullscreen):
  "Episode done! Quick quiz on what you just watched?" + "~12 questions · 3 min".
- Actions: **Start quiz** · **Not now** (this episode only) · **Don't ask again**
  (sets `avcEpisodeQuizOff`).
- Does not pause the video. Leaving the session (next episode autoplays) removes it.

### The quiz
- Start pauses the video, hides the word card, and shows a modal over the player
  with a loading state, then questions one at a time with progress "3 / 12".
- Generated by `POST /api/ai/episode-quiz` (`mode: "generate"`) through the
  background worker (`avc-episode-quiz` message).
- Question kinds: `meaning` (a word from the episode → its meaning), `line`
  (a line → what it meant), `story` (what happened), `build` (put tiles in
  order to make a sentence from the episode using words the learner knows).
  Multiple choice = 4 options; build = tap tiles in order, Undo/Clear.
- After each answer: right/wrong + one-line explanation, then Next.
- 10–15 questions; server rejects fewer than 8 valid questions (`quiz_too_short`).
- Errors (not linked, quota, network) show a plain message and a Close button;
  Close resumes nothing automatically.

### Results ("judge")
- Score "9 / 12" with a verdict band: ≥ 90% "Sharp", ≥ 70% "Solid",
  ≥ 40% "Getting there", else "Rewatch-worthy".
- Review list: each missed question with the right answer.
- **Flashcards:** "Add N missed words to reviews" — every missed `meaning`
  question's word goes into the deck as `learn` via `storage.judgeWord`, with
  the episode line as its source. Words already in the deck are not re-added
  (button counts only new ones); the button disables after use.
- **Sentence practice (Haru, Pro/Max):** "Make your own sentence with: X, Y" —
  learner types a sentence; Haru (`mode: "haru"`) returns a verdict, a corrected
  sentence, a note, and a short spoken line (TTS through the existing
  `avc-tts`). Free users see a locked Haru card with a Pro link (Pro surface
  `ext_episode_quiz`, counted `pro_prompt_shown`/`clicked`).
- **Haru reads aloud (Pro/Max):** a speaker button on `line`/`build` questions
  reads the Japanese line. Not shown for free.
- **Continue:** "Next episode" clicks the site's next-episode button when one
  exists (Netflix / Crunchyroll / YouTube), otherwise resumes playback if the
  video has not ended, then closes.

### Server (`web/src/app/api/ai/episode-quiz/route.ts`, `web/src/lib/episode-quiz.ts`)
- Auth: web session or extension sync token (`resolveProfile`); 401 otherwise.
- `generate`: any plan; reserves 1 call on the `ai` meter (refunded on failure);
  429 `ai_quota_exhausted` when out. Input clamped (≤ 120 lines × 200 chars,
  ≤ 40 known words, title ≤ 120). < 12 lines → 400 `too_few_lines`.
- `haru`: plan must be pro or max (or owner) else 403 `pro_required`; reserves 1
  `ai` call. Sentence ≤ 200 chars, required.
- Model output is validated: malformed questions dropped, choices deduped,
  answer index in range, build tiles 2–8 and joined tiles non-empty, max 15.
- Each call recorded with `recordLlmCall` (operation `episode_quiz` / `episode_quiz_haru`).

### Telemetry
- `quiz_offered`, `quiz_started`, `quiz_completed` added to the learning-loop
  allowlist on both sides (pinned by the existing mirror test).
- `ext_episode_quiz` added to PRO_SURFACES on both sides.

### Must not conflict with
- N5 course Listening (skipped while a lesson is followed).
- Sign-in gate (#206): nothing runs signed out.
- Review prompt (popup-only), agent word card (hidden while quiz is open),
  Subtitle Lens (paused video emits no lines), Settings/options (no new Settings
  field; the opt-out is its own storage key).

## Unit Tests
- `test/episode-quiz.test.ts`
  - `EpisodeLog` dedupes, caps at 400, resets on new session, samples evenly.
  - `isEpisodeEnding` — ended; near end (≤150 s / ≤10%); short clips never; mid-episode no.
  - `shouldOfferQuiz` — each blocking condition (opt-out, asked, course, few lines, little playback).
  - `scoreQuiz` / `verdictFor` — bands and build-order grading.
  - `missedWords` — only missed `meaning` questions, deduped, skips words already in deck.
  - `rememberAsked` keeps newest 50.
- `web/src/lib/episode-quiz.test.ts`
  - `normalizeQuizRequest` clamps and rejects (too few lines, bad mode, haru without sentence).
  - `sanitizeQuiz` drops malformed questions, dedupes choices, bounds answer, caps 15, too-short error.
  - `sanitizeHaru` requires verdict, trims fields.
- `test/feature-events.test.ts` (existing) still passes with new events/surface mirrored.

## Integration / Functional Tests
- Route: free user `haru` → 403 `pro_required`; free `generate` with mocked
  OpenAI → 200 and questions; quota exhausted → 429; OpenAI error → 502 and refund.
- Background relays `avc-episode-quiz` to the route with the sync token.
